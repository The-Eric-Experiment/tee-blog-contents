# Flight Log

<img src="/contents/public/airplane-1.gif" alt="Aeroplane" align="left">

This is my personal flight log of all the flights I've taken.

Since I was a kid, I had always wanted to fly on an airplane. When I was 27 I finally got to do it when I moved to Australia. Since then I've been keeping track of all of the flights I've taken.
Initially I just had origin and destination in an excel spreadsheet, but I decided to comb through my old emails, plane tickets, google maps and photos to figure out all what flights and aircrafts I've flown on, some of it was lost completely, but I got the vast majority of them.
