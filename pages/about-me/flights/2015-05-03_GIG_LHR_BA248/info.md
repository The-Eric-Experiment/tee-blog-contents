This was my first flight ever. Yeah, first time I got on an airplane was to go halfway around the world, when I moved to Australia. This was very exciting for me because I had always wanted to fly and travel overseas.

One funny thing about this flight is that, the week prior to it, I was binging episodes of "MAYDAY! Air Crash Investigation" (I'm obsessed with stories about aviation incidents for some reason) so because of that and the fact I wasn't used to airplane movements and noises, about 3 hours into the flight when I was settled in and trying to take a nap, I'd wake up panicking a bit when the plane shook a bit. I'd relax after seeing that everyone else was calm but that happened more times than I'd like to admit.

On a different note, I could not find the tail number for the aircraft used in this flight, so I have no information about it.
